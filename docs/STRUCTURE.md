## 📊 Organization Principles

### By Audience

- **Developers**: `getting-started/`, `guides/`, `developer/`
- **DevOps/SRE**: `operations/`, `deployment/`
- **Architects**: `architecture/`, `specifications/`
- **Product**: `product/`, `use-cases/`

### By Purpose

- **Learning**: `getting-started/`, `guides/`
- **Reference**: `api/`, `reference/`
- **Operations**: `operations/`, `deployment/`
- **Features**: `features/`

### By Status

- **Current**: All main folders
- **Historical**: `archive/historical/`
- **Obsolete**: `archive/obsolete/`
