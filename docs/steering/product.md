# Product Vision - Anvil Backend

**Version**: 1.0  
**Date**: January 2, 2026  
**Status**: Active

## Vision Statement

**Anvil** es la plataforma de **Multi-Agent DeFi Intelligence** que democratiza el acceso a herramientas profesionales de trading y análisis DeFi a través de una interfaz conversacional inteligente.

---

## Product Pillars

### 1. AI-First Experience

- **18 Agentes Especializados**: Cada agente es experto en un dominio DeFi específico
- **Intent Detection**: Comprensión natural del lenguaje para routing inteligente
- **Multi-Agent Workflows**: Coordinación de múltiples agentes para tareas complejas

### 2. Comprehensive DeFi Coverage

- **Portfolio Management**: Tracking y análisis de activos cross-chain
- **Market Intelligence**: Precios en tiempo real, sentimiento, predicciones
- **Trading Tools**: Swap, arbitrage, flash loans, MEV protection
- **Risk Analysis**: Scoring de riesgo, alertas, compliance

### 3. Professional-Grade Tools

- **ULTRA System**: Flash loans, arbitrage automation, MEV protection
- **Hunter AI**: Sentiment analysis, price predictions, pattern detection
- **GraphRAG**: Protocol relationship analysis, semantic search

### 4. Enterprise Ready

- **Security**: Multi-layer authentication, wallet-based auth
- **Compliance**: AML/KYC integration capabilities
- **Scalability**: Horizontal scaling, circuit breakers, retry system
- **Observability**: Full telemetry, monitoring, admin dashboards

---

## Target Users

### Primary: Active DeFi Traders

- **Profile**: Traders con portfolios $10K-$1M
- **Needs**: Real-time data, trading signals, risk management
- **Pain Points**: Information overload, multiple tools, manual analysis

### Secondary: DeFi Newcomers

- **Profile**: Crypto holders exploring DeFi
- **Needs**: Education, guidance, simplified interface
- **Pain Points**: Complexity, fear of mistakes, lack of knowledge

### Tertiary: Institutions

- **Profile**: Funds, DAOs, treasuries
- **Needs**: Compliance, multi-sig, advanced analytics
- **Pain Points**: Reporting, audit trails, team coordination
